Case Study

A previous build that matches their pain

Previous Client Build
Problem
  • Tens of thousands of checkouts started per month, ~68% abandoned
  • Generic recovery emails were converting at 2.1%
  • Founder pulling abandonment reports from Shopify manually every morning
Solution
  • Shopify webhook fires the moment a checkout is created
  • Smart wait period before any recovery action, to avoid email fatigue
  • Workflow re-queries Shopify to confirm the cart is still abandoned
  • 🎉 OpenAI generates a personalised recovery email using cart contents and customer history
  • Email sent via SMTP, every result logged for reporting
Result
  • Lifted recovered-revenue conversion from 2.1% to 11.4% and recovered $47k of abandoned cart value in the first 6 weeks.
